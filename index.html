A New Culture Shift

MAMG (Me And My Guy) isn't just a party, it's a pop-up culture. We host bring-your-crew experiences across cities in Nigeria, one city at a time, giving every crew a reason to show up together and show out. Calabar is the first stop. Show Up & Show Off is chapter one of something built to travel — and this is only the beginning.

Bring your crew. Show up, show off, and be part of the first daylight rave the city remembers.
